About Virgo & VirgoCX

VirgoCX (under Virgo Group) is a trusted trading platform providing easy and affordable access for all levels of traders to buy and sell crypto and digital assets. Virgo Group is expanding globally — including Australia, UK, and US — to become the full-service solution for all things cryptocurrency.

Website Revamp

When I started at VirgoCX, one of my key projects was revamping the main website to align with the company's new branding, including updated styles, logos, and brand colors. The revamp went beyond simply updating colors and layouts — I collaborated closely with the design lead, product manager, and head of product to gradually redesign the site, starting with the homepage and then moving to subpages.

A major focus was creating a user-friendly interface, which involved extensive research on competitors, analyzing the logic behind their user flows, and iterating designs to optimize clarity and usability. We standardized UI components such as buttons, headers, and forms to establish a cohesive design system, ensuring consistency and scalability across the platform. Accessibility considerations, including color contrast and responsive design, were integrated to make the site usable for all users.
